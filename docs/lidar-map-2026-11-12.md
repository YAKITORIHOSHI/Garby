## Root cause: the SDK cut every scan short

With `fixed_resolution:=true`, the YDLIDAR SDK sizes each scan once at startup from a mean
point count rounded to tens (`Single Fixed Size: 660`, later run `690`). When a turn has
more points than that, it truncates the surplus and prints a warning to stdout, which the
launcher discards by default (`GARBY_LIDAR_DRIVER_LOGS=0`), so nobody saw it:

```
Real points 666 > fixed points 660                     (06:09, 5.98 Hz)
Real points 694..701 > fixed points 690   x158 in 30 s (08:00, 5.74 Hz)
```

So 4-11 points were dropped from the end of every revolution, and the remaining points
were binned into fewer slots than there were points, so neighbours collided. Isolated
re-measurement (same scene, minutes apart, `logs/probe-fixed-resolution-*.txt`):

| `fixed_resolution` | Scan length | Truncation warnings | Valid returns per scan |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| true (before) | 690 fixed | 158 | 557 |
| false (now) | 694-700, follows motor speed | 0 | 578 |

`start_lidar_driver` now passes `fixed_resolution:=false` (`GARBY_LIDAR_FIXED_RESOLUTION=1`
restores the old behaviour). Every consumer already reads `angle_increment`, and the
ray-geometry caches now hold 32 scan lengths instead of 4, so the varying length
(at most 1 ms per scan if it missed) stays cached.

## The LiDAR map (owner's request)

The eight number cards and the circular-scan toggle are replaced by one radar-style map:

- Robot-centred, front up, dark scope with range rings and dashed sector boundaries.
  Front, front left, left, rear left, rear, rear right, right and front right are
  written around the scope on their own sides, in whole centimetres.
- Each sector's stop distance is a dashed arc (front 95, front diagonals 60, rear 35,
  sides 22 cm). A sector inside it reads "close" in red and its arc turns solid red.
  A read-only (bypassed) group reads "read only" in amber and its arcs are hidden.
  Arcs under 6 px at the chosen range are hidden.
- **Loading**: a green, clockwise radar sweep with a fading trail, titled with the
  supervisor's phase (Searching for LiDAR, Starting LiDAR, Waiting for first scan,
  Reconnecting LiDAR) and the seconds waited. It animates only while the map is on
  screen, at 20 frames/s, and stops on close.
- **LiDAR not available**: shown when the driver supervisor reports a failure (no
  port streaming LiDAR data, the driver exiting, no usable scan for 15 s) or after
  45 s without a scan. The reason is written in the scope. It stays up while the
  supervisor retries, so it does not flicker back to loading every retry, and
  a live scan clears it.
- **Scan needs attention**: a fresh scan flagged unusable (too few valid returns).

The supervisor's phase reaches the monitor as `read_health()["lidar_driver"]`. It is
presentation only: scans and their age alone decide STOP/GO, as before.
