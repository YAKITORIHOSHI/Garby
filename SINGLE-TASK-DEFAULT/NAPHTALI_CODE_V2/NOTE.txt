GARBY ESP32 EXECUTOR - SAFETY AND TUNING NOTES
================================================

Scope
-----
This folder is the motion executor. The BLE receiver remains responsible for
filtering LiDAR zones and calculating the direction, duration, and intensity of
the N:<ms>:<intensity>|<direction> command. The executor validates, bounds, and
smoothly applies that command; it does not receive raw LiDAR measurements.

Current safe defaults
---------------------
- CPU: 160 MHz. Hardware-timed step pulses do not require forcing 240 MHz.
- Straight cruise: 5,600 steps/s (about 0.71 m/s with the configured wheel).
- Caution speed inside 90 cm: 3,900 steps/s (about 0.50 m/s).
- Ultrasonic stop: two readings at or below 60 cm.
- Immediate ultrasonic stop: one reading at or below 25 cm.
- The confirmed 60 cm ultrasonic stop latch is enforced during turns and short
  adjustment moves as well as long straight segments; 25 cm remains the
  one-sample emergency path.
- Resume: three readings at or above 72 cm, plus a fresh LiDAR GO stream.
- Ultrasonic cadence: 40 ms, interrupt-captured (no pulseIn in the motion loop).
- Path watchdog: 800 ms. Motion requires two fresh GO packets.
- Nudge: 5-24 percent wheel-speed cut, maximum 110 ms, 220 ms settle time.
- Stale nudge commands older than 250 ms are discarded.
- Repeated Pi `[RESET]` relays are idempotent during `RETURNING`; they cannot
  abort the route and produce an early `[IDLE]` completion.

Why the servo stays centered while moving
------------------------------------------
The previous wide sweep left the ultrasonic sensor looking away from the travel
direction for most of a fast hallway run. At the same time, the old servo settle
estimate could sample before the horn reached its requested angle. The executor
now parks the sensor at DEFAULT_VIEW during every move and turn, giving continuous
front-path coverage. scanAngle() is retained for stopped/manual diagnostics.

Wheel calibration
-----------------
MOTOR1_TRIM_PCT and MOTOR2_TRIM_PCT are deliberately 0.0 until measured. To
calibrate, disable remote nudges, mark a straight 3-5 m lane, run at cruise speed,
and measure lateral drift. Change only one trim in 0.25 percent steps, repeat in
both travel directions, and keep the smallest value that removes repeatable
mechanical bias. LiDAR intensity then corrects changing corridor error rather
than masking a permanent wheel mismatch.

Required chassis tests before unattended use
---------------------------------------------
1. Confirm DEFAULT_VIEW=80 points exactly along the chassis centerline. Adjust
   DEFAULT_VIEW before changing distance thresholds.
2. Verify the ultrasonic ECHO signal is limited to ESP32-safe 3.3 V. Many 5 V
   modules require a divider or level shifter.
3. Walk into the path at cruise speed from several angles. Confirm caution speed
   begins near 90 cm and the chassis stops without crossing the 60 cm boundary.
4. Repeat with dark clothing, soft bags, narrow poles, glass, and angled walls.
   Ultrasonic and LiDAR have different blind cases; neither replaces a bumper.
5. Test unplugged/restarting RasPi and BLE bridge. The wheels must decelerate to a
   stop within the 800 ms executor watchdog and remain stopped until two GO frames.
6. Record left/right LiDAR values and commanded nudge intensity on a long hallway
   run. If it oscillates, increase receiver deadband/confirmation first. If it
   responds too slowly, increase proportional intensity in small steps; do not
   raise the executor's 24 percent cap until measured chassis testing supports it.
7. Perform a 30-60 minute loaded thermal run. Log RasPi temperature/throttling,
   executor resets, UART stale stops, motor-driver temperature, and supply dips.

Validation performed
--------------------
PlatformIO CI compilation for board esp32dev succeeded with ESP32Servo 3.2.1,
FastAccelStepper 1.2.7, and HX711 0.7.5. Hardware-in-the-loop and hallway tests
are still required because compile validation cannot establish physical braking,
sensor aiming, power integrity, or actual wheel mismatch.
