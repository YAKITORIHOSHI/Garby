ESP32-S3 BLE bridge with calibrated MPU heading (current: NAV-20261111, 2026-11-11)

Design, calibration and bench steps: docs/navigation-2026-11-11.md (system root).
Flash this sketch and MAIN_EXECUTOR_CODE together, and deploy the whole RasPi/
directory: the bridge<->executor UART lines changed (M without offset, new W,
GO:SLOW, STOP:TURN; no N: taps). An old executor rejects the new M lines and an
old Pi sends no T= turn clearance (turns then hold STOP:TURN_UNKNOWN).

Source target: ESP32-S3 Dev Module (esp32:esp32:esp32s3), 8 MB flash, PSRAM off.
This does not identify a currently selected/connected board. Keep NimBLE-Arduino;
Wire is built into the ESP32 core.

Wire while unpowered; use GPIO labels, not physical header positions:
  MPU VCC -> compatible 3.3V breakout supply; GND -> S3 GND
  SDA -> S3 GPIO8; SCL -> S3 GPIO9; pull-ups must be at 3.3V
  AD0 -> GND (0x68); 0x69 also detected; INT/XDA/XCL unused
  S3 GPIO17 TX -> main ESP32 GPIO18 RX
  S3 GPIO18 RX <- main ESP32 GPIO19 TX; common ground; 115200 baud
  S3 GPIO19/20 remain available for native USB.

BOOT: keep the robot still and level, MPU +Z up, for about 3 s. 200 fresh samples:
gravity, spread and offset are checked; the gyro bias vector and the gravity
direction are stored. Yaw rate = calibrated gyro vector . measured up axis, times
GYRO_SCALE (MpuHeading.h; 1.0 until measured, see calibration step 2). A failed
calibration holds STOP:MPU and retries by itself.

HEADING: one continuous reference from boot, streamed to the executor every 20 ms:
  M,<seq>,<heading cdeg>,<rate cdeg/s>,<epoch>,<allow>*<xor>
<epoch> changes only when the reference does (boot, recovery after more than
500 ms blind, or R on this console while stopped). Segment notices never re-zero it.
Without a valid heading it sends MPU:FAULT every 200 ms and every path packet
becomes STOP:MPU. A single bad read is skipped; 100 ms without good data faults.
Bias is re-measured while the wheels are held (STOP latched >= 1 s, or the executor
reporting IDLE / OUTBOUND COMPLETE / ROUTE FAULT >= 1 s; never in a turn), at most
0.2 deg/s per 1.5 s window and 1 deg/s from boot.

LIDAR: per new acquisition A of an S frame the bridge (a) sets the <allow> bits
from nearest clearance: CF or F <= 60 cm or a wall median <= 22 cm -> no steering;
a side with CL, L, FL or BL (CR, R, FR, BR) <= 40 cm -> no steering toward it, and
(b) forwards the wall line fit to the executor with its total age:
  W,<acq>,<age ms>,<flags>,<left mm>,<right mm>,<heading cdeg>*<xor>
Malformed or out-of-order frames withdraw permission; a repeated A refreshes
nothing. STOP, operator stop and turns withdraw permission. P frames: F=W gives
GO:SLOW; T= must be C between [TURN:START] and [TURN:END] (STOP:TURN otherwise).
Reverse parking (2026-11-16, PARK-20261116): between [BACK:START] and [BACK:END]
GO needs R=C and B=C (F is not consulted) and is sent as GO:BACK; STOP:BACK or
STOP:BACK_UNKNOWN otherwise. The scope is unknown after a bridge reboot until the
executor replays it. Flash together with executor PARK-20261116.

CONSOLE (USB 115200): H prints heading, rate, bias, tilt and epoch (yaw-sign bench
check: turn the robot left by hand, heading must rise). R requests a new reference
while stopped. The Pi log gets [BRIDGE DIAG ...] and, 2 s later,
[BRIDGE MPU VALID|INVALID bias= tilt= refresh= epoch= walls=].

Tunables: SteeringAssist.h (permission, timing), MpuHeading.h (MPU). Centring
tunables are in the executor's HeadingControl.h.

2026-10-30 BLE/manual-start repair:
New START commands rejected by UART backpressure now report UART_BUSY
to the Pi. START STATUS:<id> (inside square brackets) only queries the
executor result; it never repeats a START. If a query cannot cross the
transport, it does not invent a rejection of an already accepted start.
The Pi repeats only result queries, within its existing five-second deadline.
Executor MOTION diagnostics expose MPU/path/local-sonar holds on the Pi.
Upload both updated sketches and restart the updated RasPi runtime together.

2026-10-30 motor/BLE follow-up:
Firmware ID MOTORBLE-20261030 is sent after the Pi/controller handshake, along
with the boot reset reason. Serial logs also identify reset reason, heap,
negotiated BLE parameters, disconnect reason/age and notification queue failures.
NimBLE 2.5.1 notifications now use the explicit data-and-connection overload:
the no-data overload schedules a future characteristic read, which could read
the final fragment repeatedly after the fragmentation loop overwrote the value.
This preserves whole START/return outcomes and status lines at the default MTU.
TX power uses an explicit 9 dBm value; the ESP-IDF ESP_PWR_LVL_P9 enum was numeric
12 and does not match NimBLE's dBm-based setPower() argument.
These corrections do not establish a physical disconnect cause. Watchdog,
stale-scan, obstacle, UART and MPU holds remain active. A source/build pass does
not update a powered standalone board; upload the new source to deploy it.

2026-11-04 link and heading resilience (LINKFIX-20261104C):
An MPU fault or failed boot calibration holds STOP:MPU, then recalibrates while still and
resumes with no console or reset. [BRIDGE DIAG] mpu=<now>/<last fault> names the cause.
[MCU ALIVE] is the controller's recovery ping during a route and keeps the heading;
[MCU READY] means the controller restarted and drops it. About 2 s after each Pi
connection, MPU fault or link-parameter change the bridge sends one [BRIDGE DIAG ...]
line that appears in the Pi log. fw= shows what is really flashed. reset=BROWNOUT or a
small up_s after a drop points at supply, not radio; reset=SOFTWARE is the monitor's
Reboot button. See docs/ble-nudge-review-2026-11-04.md.
