peripherals, RTOS, and time. It verifies parser/latch/route behavior, not physical
step timing, deceleration, echo waveforms, real radio events, or electrical safety.
Its connected-system tests pass real bridge output into the controller parser
and real controller acknowledgements back into the bridge.

Key verified behaviors: independent startup/clearance gates; stale and sequence
STOP; telemetry injection rejection; correct side-avoidance direction; cooldown
preserving tap duration; both-wheel restoration; paired motor rejection; raw
close-echo STOP; no-echo latch preservation; continuous sensing during a blocked
wait; resumption of the original target; BLE reconnect recovery; matching return
outcomes; duplicate return idempotency; and incomplete-route rejection.

coalescing across sequence wrap, serialized GATT writes, stale-client callbacks,
