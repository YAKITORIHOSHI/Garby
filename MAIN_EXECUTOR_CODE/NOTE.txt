2026-10-29: Motor execution and steering review

Motor pins, calibrated route step counts, 5600 Hz maximum straight speed,
3000 Hz turn speed and 4800 step/s^2 acceleration remain unchanged.

Forward segments with steering enabled now preserve a correction's wheel-step
difference by adjusting both final wheel targets equally and oppositely. Their
sum stays fixed, so the commanded mean route distance stays unchanged. This
prevents the slower wheel from undoing the heading correction when it catches
up to an old equal-wheel endpoint. Adjustments are batched after the speed
restoration ramp; a STOP captures them after braking and resumes those targets.
New taps are suppressed within the calculated braking/tap/restoration margin.
Short calibration bumps, turns and reverse movement do not start MPU steering.

GARBY_VERBOSE_DIAGNOSTICS defaults to 0 in MAIN_EXECUTOR_CODE.h. Routine nudge,
straight-start and full modem command/response dumps are disabled by default.
Startup messages, state transitions and fault reports remain available. Set
the macro to 1 only when those extra diagnostic dumps are needed. UART ACK,
heading lifecycle and safety/status protocol frames remain enabled.

A rejected continuous wheel-start command now stops both wheels, as targeted
wheel-command rejection already did. No hardware timing or electrical issue
has been proven by these software tests. FastAccelStepper position is a
commanded pulse count; without wheel encoders it cannot detect a stalled motor.

Intense motor sound with no wheel motion needs a hardware isolation test. With
the wheels raised, use the separate MotorsConfig 400 Hz/800 step/s^2 test and
verify each wheel independently before running the calibrated route. Check
motor coil pairs, driver enable/current/microstep configuration, supply voltage
under load, common logic ground and STEP/DIR waveforms at the driver. Do not
infer a successful physical move from an increasing software position count.

2026-10-30 executor/BLE start repair:
Setup returns to the safety/recovery loop immediately after hardware setup;
the old eight-second BLE handshake wait is removed. Readiness retries and the
first-link ready beep are serviced from the normal UART/control path.

[START STATUS:<id>] queries the last manual-start result without starting a
route, consuming a request ID or refreshing path permission. It returns the
remembered ACK/failure or NOT_RECEIVED plus current state. A new start with an
uninitialized motor channel is rejected as MOTORS_UNAVAILABLE.

[MOTION:<reason>] reports path/MPU/local-sonar/motor/route holds to the Pi on
changes and status requests. This is diagnostic feedback only. Manual start
still bypasses scale/bin triggers only; all existing motion gates remain.

2026-11-04: during a route, UART silence now sends [MCU ALIVE], not [MCU READY].
READY made the bridge treat every BLE outage over 3 s as a controller restart and
silently end steering for the rest of the hallway. IDLE still sends READY because
its startup handshake needs renewing. Flash both sketches together: an older bridge
ignores ALIVE, so after its own reboot it would not re-handshake. Parked at the
destination or a route fault, status polls drop to 2 Hz. A lost MPU reference now
reports MPU:HEADING_LOST rather than a LiDAR block.

2026-11-11 navigation redesign (build NAV-20261111; supersedes the tap and step-turn
notes above). Design, calibration and bench steps: docs/navigation-2026-11-11.md.
Flash together with BLE_Receiver-Final and deploy the whole RasPi/ directory.
- Turns are MPU degrees (90/90/90/90/180, absolute along the route), tolerance 1 deg,
  then aligned to the LiDAR walls (two agreeing scans, within 8 deg of the command);
  the measured hallway direction becomes the route frame. Every turn reports
  [HEADING TURN cmd= gyro= err= align= wall_before= wall_after= spins= side= spd=].
- Which motor is the left wheel is a guess (MOTOR1_IS_LEFT_GUESS); the first turn
  checks it and swaps it once if the spin starts the wrong way.
- Straights longer than FINISH_STEPS steer continuously (no N: taps). With
  isNudgeEnabled the executor fuses the W wall lines: it steers to the measured
  hallway direction plus a bounded centring term (deadband 2 cm, engage 5 / release
  2 cm, max 5 deg). Reports [HEADING FUSE ...] every 3 s and [HEADING STRAIGHT ...].
- No motion without heading: a route is refused without the M stream, a turn is never
  finished by step count, a straight brakes when the stream stops (route fault after 10 s).
- GO:SLOW (LiDAR caution zone) drops the straight to CAUTION_SPEED; 3 plain GO restore it.
- Tunables: HeadingControl.h. Unverified constants: STEPS_PER_REV/WHEEL_DIAMETER_MM
  (used only for lateral dead reckoning) and DEFAULT_STEPS_PER_DEG (learned per turn).
Source implemented, host tested and compiled; not uploaded or floor tested.

2026-11-12 (build NAV-20261112): holds that resume, sonar sweep, low power, buzzer,
pre-run centring. Report: docs/obstacle-sweep-hold-2026-11-12.md.
- Obstacle/path holds never fail a route. waitUntilMotionPermitted() holds until fresh
  GO, no STOP and no sonar obstacle; a STOP at any moment holds again. Only an explicit
  reset, the operator stop, a latched fault, a rejected wheel command, a wrong-way or
  step-limit turn, or 5 minutes without the MPU stream (HEADING_RECOVERY_MS, was 10 s)
  fail a segment. After a heading outage over 10 s with a new S3 reference, two wall
  scans must confirm the route direction before moving on.
- Sonar (2026-11-14): reads only to ULTRASONIC_MAX_RANGE_CM = 110 cm; the ping gives up
  after about 6.6 ms and a further echo is clear. A forward stop beyond 25 cm needs two
  consecutive echoes inside 60 cm (one stray echo used to brake). Continue run after an
  emergency stop: RouteResume.h, step lists in pointsRun.ino, test_continue_run.cpp.
- Sonar: a ping with no echo inside the timeout (18 ms before 2026-11-14) is a far clear reading (it was
  "unknown" and could never release a hold in an open hallway). The servo sweeps
  (SonarSweep.h): CRUISE forward/+25/forward/-25 while driving; SURVEY forward between
  +-25/+-50/+-70 while stopped in a route; forward only while spinning, idle or parked.
  Inside a turn only the 50 cm spin circle counts; the robot surveys before each spin.
  [SONAR SWEEP ...] on USB every 5 s while surveying; [SONAR:SILENT]/[SONAR:FAULT] to the Pi.
- Low-power hold after 10 s without clearance evidence: CPU 80 MHz, 2.5 Hz status polls,
  slow pings with a survey every 4 s, [POWER:LOW]; first clear evidence -> normal power.

- Buzzer: one persistent task. Route driving: 90 ms beep every 1.2 s; held: double chirp
